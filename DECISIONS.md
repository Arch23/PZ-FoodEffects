# Decisions

| ID | Date | Subject | Decision | Reasoning / alternatives | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FE-001 | 2026-11-24 | Documentation is the project source of truth | Maintain the six root documents and the collaboration protocol. | Supports handoff between human developer, ChatGPT, and Codex. | Proposed |
| FE-002 | 2026-11-24 | Compatibility baseline | Target Build 42, SP/MP, existing saves, safe removal, and compatible nutrition-bearing third-party foods. | Required by project brief; implementation architecture is not yet selected. | Proposed |
| FE-003 | 2026-11-24 | Effect model | Use temporary, expiring food effects; numerical values, stacking, and stat targets remain undecided. | Meets the design goal without committing balance/API choices before tests. | Proposed |
| FE-004 | 2026-11-24 | Duration stacking | Qualifying consumption adds nutrition-derived duration to the matching effect's remaining duration, clamped by a configurable per-effect cap; it does not increase strength. | Approved gameplay direction. One-hour contributions and a 12-hour cap remain illustrative examples, not balance decisions. | Approved |
| FE-005 | 2026-11-24 | Initial scope and deferrals | Research protein, carbohydrates, calories, seasonings/meal quality, dietary variety, and Cooking proficiency first. Keep lipids experimental; defer vitamins and illness resistance until their data and implementation paths are verified. | Keeps scope tied to local Build 42 evidence; avoids promising a vitamin API not found in the inspected files. | Approved |
| FE-006 | 2026-11-24 | Variety and Cooking goals | Reward dietary variety over a time window rather than raw meal/item count. Cooking should affect meal quality, not a permanent unrelated character bonus. | Exact categories, window, thresholds, and any quality formula require feasibility evidence and later balance approval. | Approved |
| FE-007 | 2026-11-24 | Implementation gate | Do not implement gameplay effects until a server-authoritative post-consumption path, reversible stat surface, and presentation route have been verified and reviewed. | Installed Lua proves several consumption paths but not a safe mod hook after consumption. | Approved |
| FE-008 | 2026-11-24 | Conditional adapter architecture | Use separate food, legacy-bottle, and fluid-drink adapters only if diagnostics prove them viable; normalize their reliable fields into one validation, deduplication, and effect-processing pipeline. Keep domain calculations separate from engine/authority modules. | A single universal hook is not assumed. Field availability and authoritative timing remain diagnostic questions. | Proposed |
| FE-009 | 2026-11-26 | Offline effect expiry | Effects expire as server world time advances, including while the player is disconnected. The human requests a configuration toggle if feasible; its alternative behavior and implementation remain unapproved pending feasibility and design. | Supersedes the 2026-11-24 proposal to leave offline expiry undecided. World-time expiry is the approved default; persistence and clock behavior still require verification. | Approved (default) |
| FE-010 | 2026-11-26 | Capacity-mod interoperability | Compatibility with other mods that alter carrying capacity is not a requirement; document that limitation. Support third-party foods with usable nutritional values remains a requirement. | Human prioritized food-mod compatibility and accepted a disclaimer for competing capacity modifiers. This narrows the earlier research expectation of capacity-mod interoperability; it does not waive vanilla behavior, multiplayer authority, reversible effects, or safe save/removal requirements. | Approved |
